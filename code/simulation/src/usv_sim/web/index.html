Causal forecast inspector

What could the boat predict here?

—

Only delivered measurements at or before issuance enter the predictor. Four projected exchanges test whether two consecutive misses begin within the next three. A warning is a model decision, not knowledge of the future.

Outage warnings · frozen core, held out

How good are the warnings?

Pooled over 180 fixed-route held-out scenarios. Precision = correct alerts / alerts; recall = detected outages / eligible outages; lead time = seconds between alert and outage onset. Per-run warning scores are not shown in the trace.

            Design, provenance & interpretation
            
The two boats move on a Manhattan grid, observe cell centres and exchange bounded atomic messages. Radio fades and speed disturbances are pre-generated independently of policy. There is no collision avoidance, reconnection controller, calibrated hydrodynamics, energy measurement or measured map accuracy.

            
Completion ends at full observed coverage; return and upload are excluded. Pilot seeds 0–9 selected the warning margins; evaluation seeds 1000–1029 stay separate. Both margins are frozen at 4 dB.

00Learn · how this lab works

              
Two survey boats, one intermittent radio link.

              
This dashboard replays and compares recorded simulations of two unmanned surface vehicles (USVs) that share a 240-cell seabed survey. Five coordination policies decide how much work each boat reserves before the radio fades.
