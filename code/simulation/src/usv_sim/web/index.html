Design, provenance & interpretation

The two boats move on a Manhattan grid, observe cell centers, and exchange bounded atomic messages. Radio fades and speed disturbances are pre-generated independently of policy. There is no collision avoidance, reconnection controller, calibrated hydrodynamics, energy measurement, or measured map accuracy.

Completion ends at full observed coverage; return and upload are excluded. Pilot seeds 0–9 select warning margins; evaluation seeds 1000–1029 remain separate. Both margins are frozen at 4 dB. Physical claims need independent measurements.
