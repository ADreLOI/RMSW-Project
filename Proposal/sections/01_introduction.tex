\section{Introduction}

Marine robots can support seabed mapping missions in areas where repeated human-operated surveys are expensive, slow, or risky. A team of vehicles can potentially complete such missions faster than a single vehicle, but only if the work is divided and updated effectively during execution. This makes coordination a central part of cooperative mapping.

This project investigates how intermittent connectivity affects cooperative seabed mapping. The study will start from a controlled 2D or 2.5D simulation and may later extend the vehicle model toward simplified 3D underwater motion if the initial model is too limited. The main focus is coordination under communication loss, not high-fidelity vehicle dynamics.
