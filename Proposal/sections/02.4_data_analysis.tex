\subsection{Planned Data Analysis}

The main figure will plot mission completion time against mean outage duration, with separate curves and uncertainty intervals for the fixed-partition, reactive, and prediction-aware strategies. The primary analysis will use paired per-run differences between prediction-aware and reactive coordination under the same scenario and communication trace. Effect sizes and paired bootstrap confidence intervals will be reported for the primary comparison, with any additional statistical tests treated as exploratory.

Missions that do not reach the agreed coverage threshold before the timeout will be reported through completion proportion and coverage at timeout, not assigned an artificial completion time. Secondary results will compare redundant observations, messages and bytes, and travel distance. Predictor confusion counts, pre-outage exchanges, and task-allocation conflicts will check whether prediction changed the intended mechanism. Finally, one sensitivity analysis will vary the channel-correlation assumption most likely to alter the policy ranking. The question will be answered from the direction, magnitude, uncertainty, and communication cost of the paired differences, whether the predictive strategy helps, has no effect, or performs worse.
