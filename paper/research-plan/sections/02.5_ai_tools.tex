\subsection{Use of AI Tools}
ChatGPT/Codex has been used to organize the modular LaTeX project, review the research design, identify inconsistencies, locate and check bibliographic and software information, and draft and revise parts of the working plan. This assistance includes suggested methodological details and wording. It does not establish that the cited papers have been fully read, that candidate software has been reproduced, or that the proposed simulator has been validated. The authors remain responsible for the scientific choices, source verification, and submitted text. Before submission, they will review the assisted material and ensure that its use and final wording comply with the course's AI guideline.\footnote{\href{https://canvas.kth.se/courses/63857/pages/guideline-for-using-generative-ai-tools}{Canvas: Guideline for Using Generative AI Tools}.}

During implementation, intended uses include programming assistance, debugging, development of analysis scripts, source discovery, and feedback on the authors' explanations. Suggested code will be inspected and checked using deterministic scenarios and tests, and numerical summaries will be checked against saved simulation logs. Paper metadata and substantive claims will be checked against the original sources, while reused software will require licence and version checks. A record of tools, tasks, useful prompts, and verification steps will be maintained for the final report. AI output will not be treated as experimental evidence or as an authority supporting a scientific claim; both authors will review the analysis and be able to explain the method and conclusions.
