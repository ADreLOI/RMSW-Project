\subsection{Allocation of Responsibilities}
Ettore will lead the marine-use-case framing, coverage/finite-motion scenario, and first revision of Section 1, consistent with the agreed division. Andrea will lead the communication model, forecasting rules, experiment configuration, resource logging, analysis, and first revision of Section 2. Both authors will review the allocator, tests, parameter provenance, sources, results, and conclusions; each will reproduce a run and be able to explain the full method. This division is for coordination, not sole responsibility for either section.

We will hold a joint progress review each week and a joint acceptance review at every milestone in Section 3.1. A shared task list will record each task's owner, dependency, target date, status, and completion evidence, such as passing tests, a saved configuration, or an analysis output. Parallel development will use agreed interfaces for the motion, communication, and allocation components; changes affecting these interfaces or the frozen experiment settings will be discussed before integration. At each implementation or experiment milestone, the other author will rerun the relevant tests and at least one representative scenario from its saved configuration. Both authors will inspect the analysis and interpret the results together, approve the corresponding text, and revise the task list if a dependency or risk delays progress.
