\section{Discussion}
\subsection{Interpretation and Answers to the Research Questions}
For RQ1, prediction-triggered reservation reduced restricted mission time relative to the reactive short queue, but the comparison is dominated by boats exhausting work beyond radio range. It does not isolate forecast skill. Redundant coverage did not improve relative to S2, which generated no repeated observations but left most cells unobserved. For RQ2, the always-long queue had a lower mean restricted time in every condition, and fixed partitioning completed reliably without coordination traffic. The trend policy used less counted arithmetic on average than S3 but more than persistence; these costs do not establish an energy ordering. For RQ3, held-out replay showed similarly low precision, recall and F1, with nearly identical lead times. No consistent trend advantage follows from the mission or warning results.

The revised plan anticipated null effects and stalled reconnection. Increasing queue size can prevent some queue exhaustion even without a useful forecast. Moreover, in all 180 S2 raw traces the last movement occurred between $265$ and $520$ s, before the earliest slowdown at $600$ s. The unequal-progress intervention therefore never affected an active S2 mission. This explains its identical outcomes across progress conditions and limits what the primary comparison says about reacting to workload imbalance. We retained the frozen design and report this failure rather than retroactively move the slowdown or introduce recovery.

This limitation also distinguishes the study from work that explicitly plans regrouping \cite{bramblett2023epistemic} or communication-aware routes \cite{gordon2026communication}. The present controls compare reservation rules under a common allocator. They neither reproduce nor disprove those broader planning methods. The Markov process gives fade duration memory but does not supply an observable precursor to every onset, which limits the proposed forecasting mechanism.

\subsection{Validity, Limitations, and Generalizability}
The evidence concerns one grid, two start locations, one common greedy allocator, and synthetic numerical controls. The temporary slowdown is one limited imbalance case, not a general test of heterogeneous workloads. S2's complete censoring makes its restricted mean well-defined but leaves its uncensored completion-time distribution unidentified. Percentile bootstrap intervals with 30 seeds can be discrete and sensitive to a few stalled missions; the six conditions should not be pooled into a universal effect. The same seed is reused across policies and conditions, so pooled event counts are descriptive, not independent replications. Shared fade traces control exogenous randomness but cannot equalize route-dependent delivery.

Atomic batch commitment simplifies agreement and omits partial delivery, retransmission timing, contention, network headers and real distributed consensus. Ideal cell-centre sensing omits sonar, localization error and bathymetric uncertainty. The visual display represents this event model; it adds no waves, currents or vessel dynamics to the evidence. The simulator has no rendezvous or collision avoidance and is unsuitable as an operational controller. The optional $n=2.5$ sensitivity and oracle diagnostic were not evaluated in the frozen core, so robustness to those changes remains unanswered. All results require independent author reproduction and review. Software verification does not calibrate a marine environment.

\subsection{Ethics, Societal Aspects, and Sustainability}
Synthetic data contain no human-participant records. The relevant stakeholders include future survey operators and people who may rely on survey maps; an overstated claim could mislead them about system reliability. We therefore retain unfinished runs, report the strongest controls, and separate verified accounting from environmental fidelity. A stalled policy can appear cheap because it accomplishes less work, so coverage and censoring accompany resource indicators. Bytes, operation counts, laptop CPU time and process RAM do not determine radio or propulsion energy, battery endurance, or device lifetime. The controlled model can support an economical research comparison, but it cannot establish reduced emissions or environmental impact from a vessel mission. Publication of seeds, code and bounded claims permits scrutiny of those limits.

\subsection{Implications and Future Work}
A separate, explicitly revised study could add one common recovery/rendezvous rule and repeat all controls, or test a channel with independently justified observable precursors. These changes should be specified before a new evaluation rather than tuned to reverse the current ranking. Measured radio traces and vessel/sensor models would then be needed to assess external validity. The existing long-queue ablation should remain, because it reveals when reservation capacity accounts for the apparent benefit of prediction.
