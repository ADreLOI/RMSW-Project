\section{Discussion}
\subsection{Interpretation and Answers to the Research Questions}
For RQ1, S4-T reduced restricted mission time relative to S2. Every S2 boat pair stopped moving by $265$--$520$ s, before the earliest slowdown at $600$ s. Its zero redundant observations therefore came with incomplete coverage. With rendezvous on fresh seeds, S4-T still beat S2 by 7--10\%. For RQ2, S3 finished sooner on average than S4-T in every condition of both experiments. Fixed partitioning was fastest. Advance knowledge of fades under the same reservation action yielded no further time saving over S3. For RQ3, trend and persistence had similar held-out precision, recall and lead times. The extension found similar F1 for most deployable models and lower F1 for logistic regression.

Mox et al. relate packet delivery to robot geometry \cite{mox2024opportunistic}. Our intervention localized the S3 failures to two exchanges on one route. The fade process has memory but supplies no reliable precursor to every onset. Regrouping and communication-aware planners change the routes themselves \cite{bramblett2023epistemic,gordon2026communication}.

\subsection{Validity, Limitations, and Generalizability}
Da Silva et al. measure variability both within and across maps \cite{dasilva2024rendezvous}. Our 30 matched seeds per condition address variability within one grid, start pair and allocator. Reusing seeds across conditions limits pooled inference. A few stalls can move bootstrap limits substantially. S2's uncensored completion time remains unknown. Four S4-P completions and two S4-T stalls departed from the two-exchange rule.

Atomic exchange omits partial delivery, contention and retries. Cell-centre sensing and a synthetic seabed omit real sonar error, currents and collision avoidance. The extension was designed after observing the core failures. Its map errors compare simulated policies. The optional path-loss exponent $n=2.5$ remains untested.

\subsection{Ethics, Societal Aspects, and Sustainability}
The data are synthetic. Survey operators and map users could misjudge reliability if stalls were discarded. We retain them and report coverage alongside traffic and computation. Short queues can appear inexpensive because the boats stop early. Estimated processor time and recorded bytes cannot establish battery endurance or emissions.

\subsection{Implications and Future Work}
A prospective test should vary departure geometry and replay measured marine radio traces while retaining the always-long control. This would show whether a forecast can change an assignment early enough to improve coverage elsewhere.
