\subsection{Planned Data Analysis}

Time to full coverage will be right-censored at the common timeout. The main figure will plot restricted mean time to completion up to that limit against target mean fade duration (in update intervals), with curves for the fixed-partition reference, reactive coordination, and prediction-aware coordination. This summary retains unfinished missions without treating the timeout as an observed completion. The primary contrast is prediction-aware minus reactive, with 95\% paired bootstrap confidence intervals obtained by resampling matched scenario seeds.

Realized outage duration and link availability will be reported by policy alongside completion proportion and coverage at timeout. Secondary comparisons will report redundant observations, messages and bytes sent, and travel distance. Predictor false alarms and missed outages, pre-outage reservations, and allocation conflicts will test whether the proposed action changed the intended mechanism. One pre-specified distance-loss sensitivity check will assess whether the policy ranking depends on route-dependent connectivity. We will interpret the direction, size, uncertainty, and communication cost of the effect, including a null or adverse result.
